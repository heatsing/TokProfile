Yes, but only in specific circumstances.

TikTok's Profile View History can show a profile visit when the relevant accounts are eligible and both have the setting turned on. TikTok says the feature shows qualifying visits from the past 30 days.

If you turn Profile View History off, you will not see who viewed your profile, and other people will not see your profile visits through that feature.

This answer applies to TikTok's current Profile View History setting. Post View History, Story viewers, likes, comments and ordinary video view counts follow different rules.

## The short answer

Another TikTok user may see that you visited their profile when:

- You are signed in to an eligible TikTok account.
- Your Profile View History is turned on.
- The profile owner's eligible account also has Profile View History turned on.
- The visit is within TikTok's 30-day profile-view window.

TikTok currently says an account must:

- Belong to someone at least 16 years old.
- Have fewer than 5,000 followers.

The setting is off by default according to TikTok's current guidance.

If one of the required conditions is not met, the visit should not appear through Profile View History. That does not mean every other action is invisible.

## How Profile View History works

Profile View History is reciprocal.

Turning it on allows you to see qualifying accounts that visited your profile. It also means that when you visit another eligible account with the setting enabled, that account can see your visit.

The setting is not a one-way visitor list where you can see others while remaining hidden from them.

TikTok's current rules can be summarized as:

- **Both settings on:** qualifying profile visits can be visible to both accounts.
- **Your setting off:** you cannot see your qualifying visitors, and your visits are not shown through this feature.
- **Their setting off:** they do not receive the reciprocal Profile View History result.
- **Account not eligible:** the feature may not be available.
- **Visit older than 30 days:** it falls outside the stated history window.

These conditions describe the feature, not a promise that every visit will always appear. Platform availability and account context can change.

## Who can use Profile View History?

TikTok's current Help Center guidance limits access to people who:

- Are at least 16 years old.
- Have fewer than 5,000 followers.

If the Profile views control is missing, eligibility is one possible explanation. Other possibilities include an app-version, account or product-availability difference.

Do not rely on an old screenshot to find the setting. TikTok can change labels and navigation.

## How to turn Profile View History on or off

TikTok currently describes this path in the app:

1. Tap **Profile**.
2. Tap the **Menu** button.
3. Open **Settings and privacy**.
4. Tap **Privacy**.
5. Tap **Profile views**.
6. Turn **Profile view history** on or off.

TikTok may also surface Profile views from the Inbox or the profile screen for eligible accounts.

After changing the setting, confirm the displayed state rather than assuming the tap was saved.

## Profile views are not video views

Opening a creator's profile and watching one of their posts are separate actions.

### Profile View History

This concerns visits to the account's profile page. It uses the reciprocal eligibility and setting rules described above.

### Ordinary video view count

A public video can display a total view count. That number is not automatically a public list containing every viewer's account identity.

### Post View History

TikTok has a separate Post View History feature. Its current guidance also limits access to people at least 16 years old with fewer than 5,000 followers.

TikTok describes Post View History around recent posts and certain viewer or interaction conditions. It also warns that likes and comments can remain visible to the post owner even if someone turns Post View History off.

Turning off Profile View History therefore does not hide a like, comment or other visible interaction.

### Story view history

TikTok states that Story view history is separate from Post View History. Do not use the rules for profile visits to predict Story viewer visibility.

## Profile history and post history use different time windows

TikTok currently describes:

- Profile View History: qualifying visits within the past 30 days.
- Post View History: viewers associated with posts published within the recent seven-day window described by TikTok.

The different windows are another reason not to merge the two features into one “TikTok view history” rule.

A profile visit from several weeks ago and a view on a newly published post are evaluated by different controls.

## Does a private account change Profile View History?

Public/private account status and Profile View History are separate privacy concepts.

A private account controls who can follow the account and access restricted content. Profile View History controls reciprocal visibility for qualifying profile visits.

The [public versus private account comparison](/blog/public-vs-private-tiktok-accounts) explains which visibility changes with the account toggle and which controls still require a separate review.

Do not assume:

- A private account can never appear in Profile View History.
- A public account always exposes every profile visitor.
- Changing account type automatically changes the Profile View History setting.

Review both controls independently.

For a broader explanation of public identity fields and restricted content, read [What Information Is Public on a TikTok Profile?](/blog/what-information-is-public-on-tiktok).

## What happens when you like or comment?

A visible interaction can identify you even when a view-history setting is off.

Examples include:

- Liking a post.
- Commenting on a post.
- Following the account.
- Sending an interaction that TikTok displays with your account identity.

TikTok's Post View History guidance specifically notes that likes and comments can still appear as views to the other person even when Post View History is disabled.

If your goal is to avoid notifying another account, do not treat one disabled history setting as a guarantee that all interactions are hidden.

## Does viewing without a TikTok account appear in Profile View History?

Profile View History is described as an account-based TikTok feature. A person who is not signed in does not have a signed-in TikTok account identity to display in the same reciprocal list.

However, “not listed in Profile View History” is not the same as anonymous internet use.

A website, network, device or service may still process ordinary technical data such as:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Cookies or similar identifiers.
- Request time.
- Referring page.

Logged-out content availability can also vary by age, region, account settings and product behavior.

Avoid any website claiming that “no login” proves “100% anonymous” or “leaves no trace.”

For a request-by-request explanation, read [Watching TikTok Without an Account: What “No Login” Really Means](/blog/watching-tiktok-without-an-account-privacy).

## Does a third-party profile viewer notify the creator?

A third-party page and TikTok's Profile View History are not the same system.

A responsible third-party viewer should explain:

- Whether it opens TikTok directly.
- Whether it embeds TikTok content.
- Whether the user signs in.
- Which data source it uses.
- What request data it logs.
- Whether its public-data source is currently available.

If a third-party page redirects you to TikTok or you interact with embedded TikTok features, those later actions may occur in a TikTok-controlled context.

No third-party service should promise that it can prove the absence of every platform, browser or network record.

## Four practical examples

### Both eligible accounts have the setting on

Alex and Jordan are both eligible for Profile View History, and both enable it. Alex visits Jordan's profile.

Jordan may see Alex in the qualifying profile-view list. Alex accepts the same reciprocal rule for visits to other enabled, eligible accounts.

### The visitor turns the setting off

Alex turns Profile View History off before visiting.

Alex cannot use the feature to see profile visitors, and the visit is not shown to Jordan through the reciprocal Profile View History feature.

This does not hide a later follow, like or comment.

### The profile has more than 5,000 followers

Jordan's account exceeds the follower threshold described by TikTok.

Profile View History may not be available for that account, even though the profile itself remains public.

### The user watches and likes a recent post

Alex does not open Jordan's profile but watches and likes a recent post.

This is not a profile visit. The like is still a visible interaction, and Post View History has separate rules.

All examples are illustrative and depend on TikTok's current controls.

## A privacy checklist before browsing

1. Check whether your account is eligible for Profile View History.
2. Open the current TikTok setting and confirm whether it is on or off.
3. Remember that the rule is reciprocal.
4. Distinguish a profile visit from a video, post or Story view.
5. Avoid likes, comments and follows if you do not intend to send a visible interaction.
6. Do not equate signed-out viewing with complete anonymity.
7. Recheck the official setting after TikTok app updates.

This checklist reduces confusion; it cannot guarantee how every account or platform context will behave.

## Common questions

### Can someone see exactly how many times I viewed their profile?

TikTok's Help Center describes qualifying accounts that viewed a profile during the history window. It does not describe this feature as a public visit counter showing every repeat visit.

### Can I see profile visitors while hiding my own visits?

Not through the reciprocal Profile View History setting. Turning it off removes your access to the visitor list and hides your visits through that feature.

### Does blocking someone erase a past profile view?

Do not assume a past record will be removed without current official confirmation. Blocking and Profile View History are separate controls.

### Does clearing browser history remove a TikTok profile view?

Browser history and TikTok's account-level Profile View History are different records. Clearing local browser history does not provide evidence that a platform-side record was removed.

### Is Profile View History available to business accounts?

Eligibility should be checked in the current account interface. The official rule cited here focuses on age and follower count; product availability can still vary.
