# SEO Operating Rules

- Keep every published URL crawlable unless a documented review approves a change.
- Generate title, description, canonical, headings, visible answers, and JSON-LD from shared typed data.
- Canonicals must be absolute, self-referencing, and use `https://tokprofile.com` in production.
- Include every indexable page in the sitemap and expose crawlable internal links.
- Validate generated HTML, metadata uniqueness, canonical consistency, and JSON-LD before release.
- Do not change URLs, redirects, robots directives, or sitemap membership without recording the decision.
