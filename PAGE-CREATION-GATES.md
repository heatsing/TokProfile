# Page Creation Gates

A new indexable page must have:

- A distinct search intent and useful standalone purpose.
- Unique title, description, H1, direct answer, examples, FAQ, and related links where applicable.
- Valid self-canonical metadata and page-specific JSON-LD.
- Sitemap inclusion and at least two crawlable internal-link paths.
- Passing automated SEO checks and production build.

Do not publish keyword-swapped, unsupported, or filler pages.
